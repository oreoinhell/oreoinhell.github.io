# Story 4 The Curse of Atreus

## 涉及语法

>Sum: Future & Imperfect Indicative
>Possum: Present, Future & Imperfect Indicative; Complementary Infinitive(第六课)

### Sum的将来时与未完成时直陈式

非常的简单啊，直接背表就完事了，这就是不规则动词哈哈。

#### 将来时直陈式

||单数 (Singulāris)|复数 (Plūrālis)|
|:---|:---|:---|
|**第一人称 (1st Pers.)**|erō (I shall be)|erimus (we shall be)|
|**第二人称 (2nd Pers.)**|eris (you will be)|eritis (you will be)|
|**第三人称 (3rd Pers.)**|erit ( he/there will be)|erunt (they/there will be)|

#### 未完成时直陈式

||单数 (Singulāris)|复数 (Plūrālis)|
|:---|:---|:---|
|**第一人称 (1st Pers.)**|eram (I was)|erāmus (we were)|
|**第二人称 (2nd Pers.)**|erās (you were)|erātis (you were)|
|**第三人称 (3rd Pers.)**|erat ( he/there was)|erant (they/there were)|

### 不规则动词Possum, posse, potuī

#### 现在时直陈式

||单数 (Singulāris)|复数 (Plūrālis)|
|:---|:---|:---|
|**第一人称 (1st Pers.)**|possum (I am able)|possumus (we are able)|
|**第二人称 (2nd Pers.)**|potes (you are able)|potestis (you are able)|
|**第三人称 (3rd Pers.)**|potest ( he is able)|possunt (they are able)|

#### 将来时直陈式

||单数 (Singulāris)|复数 (Plūrālis)|
|:---|:---|:---|
|**第一人称 (1st Pers.)**|poterō (I shall be able)|poterimus (we shall be able)|
|**第二人称 (2nd Pers.)**|poteris (you will be able)|poteritis (you will be able)|
|**第三人称 (3rd Pers.)**|poterit ( he will be able)|poterunt (they will be able)|

#### 未完成时直陈式

||单数 (Singulāris)|复数 (Plūrālis)|
|:---|:---|:---|
|**第一人称 (1st Pers.)**|poteram (I was able)|poterāmus (we were able)|
|**第二人称 (2nd Pers.)**|poterās (you were able)|poterātis (you were able)|
|**第三人称 (3rd Pers.)**|poterat ( he was able)|poterant (they were able)|

### 补充不定式(Infinitīvus Complētīvus)

像possum,dēbeō这样的词后面需要通过不定式来补充其完整含义。涉及的词主要有possum *be able*，dēbeō *should*，audeō *to dare*，cupiō *to desire*，volō *to want*，incipiō *to begin*，soleō *be accustomed*等。

Fīliī Pelopis erant Atreus Thyestēsque. Thyestēs uxōrem Atreī corrumpit; tum Atreus vitium invenit et tolerāre nōn potest. Īnsidiās igitur contrā frātrem cōgitat: "Sum plēnus īrae! Quārē fīliōs parvōs meī frātris necābō secābōque. Tum membra coquam et Thyestae cēnam dabō." Necat Puerōs; Thyestēs suōs fīliōs mortuōs in mēnsā videt. Ō miser Thyestē! Nihil nunc habēs. Sed, ō Atreu, propter tua magna vitia fīliī tuī magnās poenās dabunt. In animīs fīliōrum tuōrum manēbit tua culpa antīqua; erit perpetua. Quid dēbēmus dē tuīs īnsidiīs cōgitāre, ō Atreu? Tuam īram nōn poterās superāre; mala igitur erit semper tua fāma. Tē tuamque vītam paucī bonī laudābunt, sed multī culpābunt.

<split>

Atreus and Thyestes were the sons of Pelops. Thyestes seduces the wife of Atreus; then Atreus finds out the crime and cannot tolerate it.
Therefore he thinks of a plot against his brother: "I am full of anger! Therefore I will kill and cut up my brother's little sons. Then I will cook the limbs and host a dinner for Thyestes." He kills the boys; Thyestes sees his dead sons on the table. Oh poor Thyestes! Now you have nothing. But, oh Atreus, because of your great evil your sons will pay a great penalty. Your ancient crime will stay in the souls of your sons; it will be perpetual. What should we think of your plot, oh Atreus? You could not overcome your anger; therefore your fame will always be bad. Few good people will praise you and your life, but many people will blame you.

## 评述

挺巧的，昨天刚刚看完诺兰版《奥德赛》今天就翻完了这篇。如果说金苹果之争是神话层面特洛伊战争的起点，那么凡人视角一切悲剧的起点可能就是这个故事了。得说拉丁语确实别有风味，用非常简单的叙述就有如此惊心动魄的效果。

如果说诺兰至少捕捉到了荷马史诗中的一部分，那一定是联军在特洛伊的暴虐。但我对由暴虐引发而来的奥德修斯反思战争非常不感冒，破坏秩序的落脚点在于破坏了Zeus Xenios又味道很怪。不如说故事卡在了一个既不足够凡人，又不足够神话史诗感的位置。这是我对诺兰《奥德赛》电影最大的意见。

至于血亲世仇……也许就是因为是血亲，所以才会有这般世仇？人们伤害最亲近的人时才会格外的无所顾忌，我想这也是阿特柔斯与提埃斯泰斯故事的启示。

最后我又在想《特洛伊妇女》了。有空得找来读读。比诺兰见缝插针的那点女性主义深刻太多。
